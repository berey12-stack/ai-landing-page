Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about creating your free business website.

Is QuickBiz Site really free?

Yes, 100% free. You generate your page here for free, then host it on GitHub Pages which is also free. There are no charges at any step.

Do I need to know how to code?

No coding knowledge needed at all. Just describe your business, pick a colour, and click Generate. We handle everything else.

Can I use this for my Instagram or Facebook shop?

Absolutely — that's exactly what QuickBiz Site is built for. Once your page is live, add the link to your Instagram bio or Facebook profile so customers can learn more about your business.

Will my page appear on Google?

Yes. Every page generated includes SEO meta tags, local geo tags, and a LocalBusiness schema so Google can understand and rank your business page — especially for local searches in your city.

What types of businesses can use this?

Any small business — bakeries, cafés, car workshops, beauty salons, eco shops, tutors, fitness trainers, food delivery, and more. We auto-detect your business type and generate relevant content.

How do I publish my page online?

Download your HTML file, create a free GitHub account, upload the file to a new repository, and enable GitHub Pages. Your site will be live at a free link you can share anywhere.
